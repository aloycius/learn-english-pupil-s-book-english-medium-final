Learn English Pupil's Book Standard One (English Medium Schools)

Letter sounds f, z, s and v, or letters f, z, s and v

Activity 1

1. Identify the sweets containing letter sound f. Or sign the numbers for the sweets containing the letter f.

2. Pronounce letter sound f from the sweets. Or fingerspell the letter f from the sweets.

Activity 2

1. Identify the sweets containing letter sound z. Or sign the numbers for the sweets containing the letter z.

2. Pronounce letter sound z from the sweets. Or fingerspell the letter z from the sweets.
